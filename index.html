📅 May 24, 2025 ✈️ General Santos → Manila → Denpasar

Day 0: Traveling from General Santos City, Philippines to Denpasar, Bali, Indonesia

We are packed and ready on our way to General Santos City International Airport (GES). I felt worried about what might happen at immigration and during our stay in Seminyak and Les. But apart from that, we were ecstatic and looking forward to all the adventures waiting for us in Bali. Upon arrival at General Santos City Airport, we took our last photo with our family for the month and headed towards the departure gate.

Once we boarded the plane, we flew to Manila International Airport (NAIA Terminal 2). The flight was remarkably smooth. After landing at NAIA, we transferred via shuttle bus to Terminal 1, enjoyed a quick lunch at Jollibee, and proceeded to immigration. The anxiety melted away once cleared, and we boarded our international flight straight into I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport in Denpasar, Bali!

📅 May 25, 2025 🏖️ Seminyak Beach & La Plancha

Day 1: Resting and Strolling at the Beachside in Seminyak

Walking through Sunset Beach in Seminyak during golden hour was pure magic. The coastline was filled with surfers riding the evening swell and travelers enjoying the vibrant atmosphere. We stopped at La Plancha, relaxed on the iconic colorful beanbags, ate dinner (wood-fired pizza, cold beer, and hummus), and watched the sun dip peacefully below the horizon.

📅 May 26, 2025 🏄 Kuta / Seminyak Waves

Day 2: Surfing Lessons and Trying Indian Cuisine

A thrilling morning catching our first waves with local surf instructors along the coast. Balancing on the surfboard against the warm tropical breaks was exhilarating. After working up a huge appetite, we explored local dining spots and savored delicious, aromatic Indian dishes and curries.

📅 May 27, 2025 🐢 Turtle Conservatory → 🚐 Travel to Les Village

Day 3: Last Day in Seminyak, Local Turtle Conservatory, and Travel to Les

We packed our bags and bid farewell to vibrant Seminyak. Before heading north, we visited a local marine turtle conservation center to learn about rescue efforts and baby sea turtles. Afterwards, we embarked on a scenic drive through Bali's lush interior hills towards the tranquil coastal village of Les in North Bali.

📅 May 28, 2025 🤝 Les Village & ACS Singapore

Day 4: Resting in Les & Cultural Immersion with ACS Singapore Students

After a restful night surrounded by nature in Les, we joined students from Anglo-Chinese School (ACS) Singapore for an enriching cultural immersion day. We shared stories, exchanged insights on student life, and experienced traditional Balinese hospitality in the village.

📅 May 29, 2025 🤿 Les Reef

Day 5: Snorkeling the Living Reefs of Les Village

Exploring the restored coral reefs in Les. The crystal clear waters revealed colorful clownfish, sea anemones, and thriving marine conservation projects run by the local community.

📅 May 30, 2025 🎓 Carroll University Arrival

Day 6: Arrival of Carroll University & Afternoon Snorkeling

Welcoming the student and faculty group from Carroll University to Les! We oriented them around the grounds and celebrated their arrival with an afternoon snorkel session along the coastline.

📅 May 31, 2025 🛕 Puseh Temple & Yeh Mampeh Waterfall

Day 7: Puseh Temple & Waterfall Expedition with Language Exchange

We journeyed to the historic Puseh Temple, honoring local customs with traditional sarongs, followed by a hike to the majestic Yeh Mampeh waterfall in Les. In the afternoon, we held an engaging language exchange session with local youth.

Balinese Temple stone carving and sacred architecture
📅 June 1, 2025 🌱 Sustainable Farming

Day 8: Family Day and Spirulina Greenhouse Construction

Hands-on sustainability work building the framework for a community spirulina greenhouse to support local nutrition and eco-farming initiatives.

📅 June 2, 2025 🌊 Beji Temple & Sekumpul Waterfalls

Day 9: Beji Temple & Sekumpul Waterfall Hike

A breathtaking trek down to the multi-tiered Sekumpul Waterfalls, surrounded by towering emerald canyons and mist. We also visited the ancient stone relief carvings of Pura Beji Sangsit.

Lush tropical terraces and valleys of Northern Bali
📅 June 3, 2025 🧘 Yoga & Marine Biology

Day 10: Morning Yoga, Snorkel Bingo, & Spirulina Fence Building

A restorative sunrise yoga session followed by an educational 'Snorkel Bingo' identifying fish species on the reef and finishing up greenhouse fencing work.

📅 June 4, 2025 🏝️ Menjangan Island National Marine Park

Day 11: Menjangan Island (Deer Island) Adventure

Boat trip to the pristine waters of Menjangan Island in West Bali National Park. World-class wall diving and snorkeling with incredible underwater visibility and wild deer roaming the beaches.

📅 June 5, 2025 🎨 Amisewaka Technical School & Coral Tables

Day 12: Silkscreen Painting, Amisewaka Tour, and Coral Propagation

A creative day doing traditional silkscreen printmaking, touring the vocational hospitality programs at Amisewaka Technical School, and transplanting live coral fragments onto underwater propagation tables.

📅 June 6, 2025 🎭 Cultural Celebration

Day 13: Organic Pesticide Making, CU Farewell, & Traditional Dances

Crafting natural botanical pest solutions with local farmers followed by an unforgettable farewell evening featuring hypnotic Balinese music and traditional Legong and Barong performances.

📅 June 7, 2025 🌋 Mount Batur & Ubud

Day 14: Mt. Batur Sunrise Hike and Journey to Cultural Ubud

Early 3:00 AM trek ascending the volcanic slopes of Mount Batur to watch the sunrise above the clouds over Lake Batur, followed by traveling to the cultural heart of Ubud.

📅 June 8, 2025 🎭 Ubud Artisan Village

Day 15: Visiting Master Balinese Mask Makers

An eye-opening visit with master woodcarvers sculpting sacred Topeng dance masks from hibiscus and pule wood in their open-air family compound.

📅 June 9, 2025 🧵 Natural Batik & Dyeing

Day 16: Scarf and Textile Botanical Dyeing Workshop

Learning ancient Indonesian indigo and plant-based fabric dye techniques to create custom patterned scarves and t-shirts.

📅 June 10, 2025 🎙️ Reflections & Return to Les

Day 17: Interviews, Final Reflections, and Journey Back North

Conducting interviews to document the insights gained during the research expedition, concluding group projects, and returning to Les village.

📅 June 11, 2025 🌅 Final Sunset in Paradise

Day 18: Final Reflections & Looking Back at Bali

Taking in the peaceful coastal breeze, reviewing journal notes and photographs, and cherishing the incredible people and culture that made this month in Indonesia so profound.
